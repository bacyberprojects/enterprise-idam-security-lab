# Organisation Overview

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Southern Cross Financial Services (SCFS)

**Industry:** Financial Services

**Location:** Melbourne, Australia

**Employees:** 50 (fictional)

**Project Type:** Identity and Access Management (IAM) Security Lab

## Business Departments

SCFS has five departments:

1. Information Technology (IT)
2. Finance
3. Human Resources (HR)
4. Operations
5. Customer Service

## Business Scenario

SCFS handles sensitive customer information, financial records and employee data. The organisation needs to ensure that employees only access the systems and information required for their job responsibilities.

The company has identified potential security risks associated with excessive user permissions, inconsistent access approvals and delayed removal of access when employees leave.

## IAM Project Objectives

1. Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
2. Apply the principle of least privilege.
3. Design a Joiner–Mover–Leaver (JML) process.
4. Conduct periodic user access reviews.
5. Identify and remediate excessive access permissions.
6. Document IAM governance and security controls.

## Project Scope

This project uses fictional employees and simulated business systems. It will demonstrate IAM governance processes and, where possible, practical identity configuration in a lab environment.

No real customer data, employee credentials or production systems will be used.
